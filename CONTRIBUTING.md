# Contributing to HeapScope

Thank you for your interest. HeapScope does not accept outside contributions yet. Pull requests from outside the
project are closed without review, whatever they change: code, tests or documentation.

## Why

HeapScope is dual-licensed: the GNU AGPL v3 with additional terms, and a commercial license (see `LICENSE`,
`ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md` and `COMMERCIAL.md`). A contribution stays its author's. Without a contributor agreement in
place, merging one would stop the project from offering that part of HeapScope under its commercial license. A
contributor agreement is planned, and this file will say when it is in place.

## What is welcome

- Bug reports and feature requests, as GitHub issues. Describe the problem in words. Please do not paste code you
  would like included, and do not attach dumps, traces or logs: they can hold your application's data.
- Security problems: report them privately, as `SECURITY.md` describes. Do not open a public issue.
- Questions about commercial licensing: rayklundt (at) outlook (dot) com.
